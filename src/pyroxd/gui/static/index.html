How to read the pyroXD console

The engine scores a live flow stream from the self-contained lab. Nothing here touches a real network.
1

Threat Timeline

Each flow's fused anomaly score (0-1) scrolls right to left. The dashed line is the alert threshold; points above it are flagged and coloured by attack family. The quiet green band is normal traffic.

2

Detector Consensus

Four independent detectors each give an opinion on the latest flow. Half-Space Trees catch odd feature combinations, EWMA catches volume spikes, and the two entropy detectors catch scans and floods. An alert fires when any credible detector is confident.

3

Distribution Entropy

Live Shannon entropy of destination ports and destination IPs over a sliding window. A sudden collapse or spike is the fingerprint of a scan, flood, or beacon.

4

Alert Stream

Every flagged flow, newest on top, with the detectors and features that drove it. Click a row to inspect the full breakdown.
